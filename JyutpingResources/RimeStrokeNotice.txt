Rime stroke keys h, s, p, n, z are translated respectively to
internal m, /, comma, period, n. The five-key UI remains unchanged.
The converted dictionary entries are
compiled into chinese-shapes.sqlite3 together with public-domain
Fcitx5 Table Extra entries. This derivative dataset is distributed
with the keyboard extension. The source data and the conversion
program (scripts/build-chinese-shapes.py) are available at
https://github.com/Diddian/Altboard-Canto .
No Rime or Fcitx program code or runtime is embedded.
